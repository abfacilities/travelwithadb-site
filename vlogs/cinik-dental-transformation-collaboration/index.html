Behind the Scenes: The Cinik Dental Transformation Collaboration

One of the most rewarding collaborations so far has been working with Cinik Dental on a behind-the-scenes travel and lifestyle series covering a real hair and teeth transformation journey.

The brief

Rather than a polished, purely promotional piece, the goal was to capture something honest — the nerves, the process, the city in between appointments, and the reveal at the end. That meant shooting across multiple days, blending documentary-style footage with cinematic travel content.

What we produced

  • Multiple short-form reels covering the patient journey from arrival to results
  • Cinematic city and lifestyle footage to give the series a travel-first feel, not just a clinical one
  • Content edited natively for both Instagram and TikTok audiences

You can watch the full series on the Reels page, including the original transformation reel and the follow-up content from Cinik Dental's own channel.
